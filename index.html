Practice

What companies hire me for.

Three kinds of work. Standards I actually audit and train: ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, ISO 22000, and ISO 17025 as an internal auditor.

01

Implementation & certification

Gap analysis, documentation, internal audits, and preparation for the certification audit. The aim is a system the organisation can run after I leave.

02

Audits & management review

ESHS and ISO 17025 internal audits, observation and non-conformance reports, and management review with the people who own the actions.

03

Lead auditor training

Training on ISO 9001, 14001, 45001 and 22000. 12+ lead auditors trained. That figure is trainees, not companies audited.
