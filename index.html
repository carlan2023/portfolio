01Practice

What companies hire me for.

Three kinds of work. Standards I actually audit and train: ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, ISO 22000, and ISO 17025 as an internal auditor.

  • ISO 9001
  • ISO 14001
  • ISO 45001
  • ISO 22000
  • ISO 17025
  1. 01

    Implementation & certification

    Gap analysis, documentation, internal audits, and preparation for the certification audit. The aim is a system the organisation can run after I leave.

  2. 02

    Audits & management review

    ESHS and ISO 17025 internal audits, observation and non-conformance reports, and management review with the people who own the actions.

  3. 03

    Lead auditor training

    Training on ISO 9001, 14001, 45001 and 22000. 12+ lead auditors trained. That figure is trainees, not companies audited.

03About

ISO work, from Kampala.

I am a mechanical engineering graduate of Makerere University. The work I do now is quality, environment, health and safety, and food safety: implement the system, audit it, and train the people who will run it.

The practice

I am Lead Auditor and Managing Director at NAM QMS (opens in a new tab). I also consult with IQS Management Systems and maintain the ISO 17025 system at Terya Construction Laboratory. Since 2023 I have audited more than 40 companies. Separately, I have trained 12+ lead auditors.

Technical work

I write software as well, including VendorVett (opens in a new tab), a vendor due-diligence product. Earlier I worked on enterprise networks. That technical work sits with the consulting.

ECAFU

I founded ECAFU Initiative Uganda, a nonprofit in Kyotera District for youth and women empowerment through education and entrepreneurship. ECAFU partners with 3 schools.
